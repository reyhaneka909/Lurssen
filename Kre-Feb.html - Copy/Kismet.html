- NORD/OPUS -

NNord is a large luxury superyacht constructed by Lürssen and recognized as one of the most visually assertive and technically advanced yachts of its generation. Characterized by its angular lines, fortress-like silhouette, and dark exterior palette, the vessel exhibits a strong military-inspired aesthetic rarely seen in private yachts. This striking exterior is deliberately contrasted by an interior environment designed to the standards of a five-star luxury hotel, featuring refined materials, expansive social spaces, and comprehensive leisure facilities. Because of this duality between its formidable appearance and lavish comfort, Nord is often nicknamed the “warrior in a tuxedo,” symbolizing the fusion of strength and elegance in modern superyacht design.
