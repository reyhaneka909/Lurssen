Lürssen (or Lürssen Werft) is a German shipyard with headquarters in Bremen-Vegesack and shipbuilding facilities in Lemwerder, Berne and Bremen-Fähr-Lobbendorf. Plant in Lemwerder Plant in Schacht-Audorf (Rendsburg) damaged by a fire in 2024 Lürssen designs and constructs yachts, naval ships and special vessels. Trading as Lürssen Yachts, it is one of the leading builders of custom superyachts such as Paul Allen's Octopus, David Geffen's Rising Sun, and Khalifa bin Zayed Al Nahyan's Azzam, the second largest private yacht in the world at 180 m in length after the REV Ocean.

Behind the lens for the Lürssen Fine Art Book.

For the first time in our 150-year history, Lürssen presents a fine art book that brings to life the craft, heritage, and lifestyle of a selection of our most celebrated yachts.

Fashion photographer Jarmo Pohjaniemi undertook a 10-month, globe-spanning journey to document the Lürssen fleet as never before. From the Arctic waters of Greenland to the turquoise coastlines of the Maldives, from the serene landscapes of Phuket to the storied shores of Europe, 36 Lürssen yachts have been captured in their natural element.

Synchronised swimmers aboard FLYING FOX. PELORUS navigating a freak storm off Cyprus. Sweeping aerial views of icons like RISING SUN, OCTOPUS, and LUMINANCE gliding through some of the most secluded waters. Pohjaniemi has beautifully captured the spirit of adventure and uncompromising vision that defines every Lürssen.

Across 300 pages of cinematic imagery and compelling narrative by luxury journalist Rachel Ingram, this limited edition book is an intimate portrait of a legacy built on care, dedication, and mastery since 1875.

Al Lussail

Al Lusail is a 123-meter ultra-luxury superyacht built by Lürssen and delivered in 2017 for the Emir of Qatar. Conceived as a floating royal residence rather than a conventional yacht, it combines monumental scale, advanced German engineering, and palace-level interior design.

Kismet

Kismet (the latest 2024 version) is the newest masterpiece from Lürssen, designed to replace its predecessor bearing the same name. This superyacht is often referred to as a “floating palace” due to its extreme level of luxury, remarkable craftsmanship, and outstanding technical sophistication.

Nord/Opus

Nord is one of the most striking and aggressively styled superyachts ever constructed by Lürssen. It is frequently nicknamed the “warrior in a tuxedo” due to its bold, military-inspired exterior design, which contrasts sharply with the refined, five-star hotel-level luxury found within its interior spaces.

Ahpo

Ahpo (now frequently referred to as Lady Jorgia) is a 115.1-meter superyacht delivered by Lürssen in 2021. The yacht was thoughtfully designed to provide maximum comfort for family living, with a strong emphasis on sports facilities, wellness amenities, and a healthy, active lifestyle on board.

F125

The F125, also known as the Baden-Württemberg-class frigate, is one of the most advanced warships operated by the German Navy. It was built by the ARGE F125 consortium, a collaboration between Lürssen and ThyssenKrupp Marine Systems, combining their expertise in naval engineering and defense technology.
